About Factory

A metal factory is a specialized industrial facility where raw metal materials are transformed into functional structures and parts. Inside the plant, heavy machinery cuts, bends, and shapes sheets or bars of metal with high precision. Workers and automated robotic systems then weld and assemble these individual components into final products. The finished metal is treated with protective coatings to prevent rust and improve long-term durability. Ultimately, these factories supply critical parts to essential global industries, including automotive, construction, and aerospace.
